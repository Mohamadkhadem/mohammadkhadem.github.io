Open-source case study

Persian Calendar Kit

A complete Flutter toolkit for Persian (Jalali/Shamsi), Gregorian, and Hijri dates—built to make date logic, localization, and polished calendar UI easier to ship.

View on GitHub ↗Explore capabilities

The challenge

Date handling for Persian-language apps is more than conversion: teams need correct calendar rules, localized digits and words, holidays, flexible formatting, and reusable interface components. This kit collects those concerns in one Flutter-first package.

What it includes

Calendar conversion

Jalali, Gregorian, and Hijri conversion, with Unix timestamps, ISO 8601, Julian days, and Tehran time support.

Date intelligence

Arithmetic, ranges, business days, age, periods, seasons, relative time, and Iranian holidays.

Formatting & parsing

Pattern-based input and output for calendars, Persian/Arabic/English digits, and dates written in words.

Ready-made Flutter UI

Calendar, week strip, grids, date and range pickers, wheel picker, and form field components.

Designed for localization

Swappable Persian, Dari, Kurdish, and English strings with RTL/LTR support and theme extensions.

Open source

MIT licensed and structured for teams that need customization without rebuilding date infrastructure.

Engineering focus

The package is designed as a flexible toolkit rather than a fixed calendar view: consumers can use the date models alone, adopt the prebuilt widgets, or replace individual pieces through themes and builders.
