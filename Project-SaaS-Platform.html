Problem & Solution

Most financial systems become difficult to scale as business operations grow. Fragmented company management, disconnected subscription logic, and rigid accounting infrastructures often lead to operational complexity. Orash addresses this by providing a centralized modular SaaS architecture that supports multi-company management, scalable accounting workflows, subscription handling, and long-term extensibility.
